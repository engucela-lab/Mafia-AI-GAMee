HOW TO PLAY

🎭 THE BASICS

Mafia Table is a social deduction game. The innocent town roles win by eliminating the Mafia. The Vigilante has one shot and may skip the kill.

🕒 GAME PHASES

DAY: Discussion and public voting. Players argue, share information, or lie.

NIGHT: Mafia discusses, then votes on one victim. The Doctor protects someone, the Sheriff investigates, and the Vigilante may use or hold one shot.

🩸 BLOOD NIGHTS (FINAL SHOWDOWN)

When only two players remain, the final vote or night action decides who controls the table.

🎨 CHARACTER CUSTOMIZATION

Use Customize for avatars. Use Settings → AI Players for each player’s name, provider, model, key, and personality.

🗣️ PRIORITY SPEAK

Each AI rates its own thoughts and message from 1–10. The scheduler reads every candidate, favors relevant answers and quiet players, and prevents back-to-back speakers.

☣️ THE PLAGUE & ROLES

The available roles are Mafia, Villager, Sheriff, Doctor, and Vigilante. The Vigilante has one shot and may skip the kill.

🔥 CHAOS & PHYSICS

Enable Chaos Mode to remove all dialogue turns; everyone screams at once! Ragdoll Physics causes players to fly around the room like tennis balls when shot or executed.

😈 TROLL PANEL

If enabled in Settings, use the Troll Panel to manipulate the simulation. You can force votes, revive dead players, silence anyone, skip phases, or even Force Speech to make bots say whatever you type.

🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS & RECORDING

Complete challenges to unlock Achievements. Enable Record Game to capture a .webm video of your match including the 3D action and dialogue.
